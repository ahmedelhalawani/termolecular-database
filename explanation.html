Explanation of Database

Concise guide to what each column means.

Goals & scope

This page explains the dataset columns and gives guidance for interpretation, units, and uncertainties.

Quick example

If a row lists $A = 1.2\times 10^{30}$, $n = -2.5$, and $E = 5.1\;\text{kcal mol}^{-1}$, then the generalized Arrhenius form is $$k(T) = A T^n e^{-E/(RT)}.$$

Column-by-column explanations

Reaction

Balanced chemical equation for the process studied. If a third body is required, it is shown as M. Radical species are denoted with a dot (•).

E / kcal mol⁻¹

Activation energy in kcal/mol. Appears in the Arrhenius exponent: $e^{-E/(RT)}$. Provided alongside the kJ value.

E / kJ mol⁻¹

Same activation energy in kJ/mol. Conversion: $1\;\text{kcal mol}^{-1} = 4.184\;\text{kJ mol}^{-1}$.

A (pre-exponential)

Frequency factor in the Arrhenius expression. Units depend on molecularity (termolecular → cm⁶ mol⁻² s⁻¹). Appears in $k(T) = A T^n e^{-E/(RT)}$.

k

Rate constant at the given temperature. For termolecular reactions: cm⁶ mol⁻² s⁻¹.

n (temperature exponent)

Exponent in $k(T) = A T^n e^{-E/(RT)}$. Accounts for deviations from simple Arrhenius behavior. For $n=0$ That means $T=1$ and is constant.

Temp. / K

Temperature (Kelvin) for which $k$ applies. If a range is shown, parameters are valid within that range.

High Pressure Reaction Rate — Column-by-column explanations

Reaction (High Pressure)

Balanced chemical equation evaluated in the high-pressure limit. Molecularity and third bodies (M) are shown when applicable.

k∞

High-pressure limit rate constant. Represents the value of the rate coefficient when pressure is sufficiently high that collisional stabilization is no longer rate-limiting.

Temp. / K

Temperature (Kelvin) corresponding to the high-pressure limit rate constant. If a range is provided, k∞ is valid within that interval

Error Limits (Δlog k)

Uncertainty in logarithmic units for the high-pressure rate constant. Δlog k = 0.3 corresponds to roughly a factor of 2 variation
